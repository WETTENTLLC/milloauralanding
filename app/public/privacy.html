Privacy Notice

WETT Girls is the music and artist website for Millo My and Aura Hall, presented by WETT Entertainment. This notice explains the information handled when you visit the website, contact us, or participate in the Fan Championship.

At a glance: The site does not ask for health information, precise location, government ID, or payment-card details. Optional Google Analytics is not loaded unless you choose to accept analytics.

1. Who operates this site

The site is operated by WETT Entertainment. For privacy questions or requests, contact millomy2@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, payment-card data, or sensitive personal information by email.

2. Information we handle

  • Basic website and security information: Our hosting, network, and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, request timestamps, and security logs to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot service issues.
  • Fan Championship registration: If you request a voting sign-in link, Supabase Auth processes your email address and account identifier. You must verify the email address to vote.
  • Voting records: The voting system stores the account identifier, voting round, selected song identifiers, and submission timestamp. The account and ballot are linked internally to enforce one ballot per verified account per round. Balloting is private from public visitors, but it is not anonymous to authorized system administrators.
  • Analytics: If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 may process website usage information and identifiers/cookies to provide audience and site-usage statistics. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded by this site.
  • Messages you send: If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate the website and voting feature, verify voting accounts, prevent duplicate or fraudulent votes, protect the site, respond to messages and requests, maintain contest records, and—only with your choice—understand aggregate website traffic.

4. Service providers and external links

The website is hosted and published through GitHub Pages. The Fan Championship uses Supabase for authentication and the voting database. When analytics is accepted, Google Analytics is used for site measurement. These providers process information to provide their services under their own terms and privacy documentation.

The site links to third-party services including Instagram, UnitedMasters, Spotify, Apple Music, and Gumroad. When you follow an external link, that provider's privacy notice and terms apply. WETT Girls does not control those external websites.

5. Cookies and analytics choices

Optional Google Analytics is off until you select “Accept analytics.” Selecting “Reject optional” keeps it off. The site stores your choice in your browser so it can remember the preference. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the site footer. You can also clear site storage through your browser settings.

6. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including operating the Fan Championship, maintaining accurate results, addressing disputes or abuse, meeting legal obligations, and keeping appropriate security records. Voting records may be retained through the completion of the relevant round and any reasonable results-review or dispute period. We periodically review whether account and ballot information is still needed and delete or de-identify it when it is no longer reasonably required, subject to legal and integrity needs.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You may reject optional analytics and may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, where applicable. We may need to verify your request before acting. Some voting information may need to be retained for a limited period to protect the integrity of an active competition, resolve a dispute, or comply with law. Privacy rights and appeal rights vary by location and applicable law. We do not discriminate against people for exercising privacy rights that apply to them.

8. Children

The website is a general-audience music and artist site, not a service designed for children under 13. Fan Championship registration is limited to people who confirm they are at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to this site's current functions, including verified-email voting, restricted access to ballot records, and access controls provided by our hosting and backend services. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed perfectly secure. We review and improve safeguards as the site evolves.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when the notice is updated. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

This notice describes current site functionality and is not a certification or legal opinion. It should be reviewed if WETT Girls adds advertising, mailing lists, payments, user profiles, new analytics, or new data collection.
